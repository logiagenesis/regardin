# Regardin Construction — 2K image generation brief

Create the following **10 images as one coordinated batch**. Return the files using the exact filenames below. These are service and brand illustrations, not documentary photographs of Regardin's completed projects. Do not generate replacement customer testimonials, before/after evidence, company accreditations or fictitious portfolio projects.

## Delivery specification — applies to all 10 files

| Requirement   | Exact specification                                                                                         |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Canvas        | **2048 × 1536 pixels**, landscape, **4:3**                                                                  |
| Delivery      | High-quality **PNG**, sRGB colour profile; one image per file                                               |
| Composition   | Edge-to-edge image. No white bars, borders, picture frames or blurred padding                               |
| Consistency   | Same restrained photographic direction, natural lighting and colour treatment throughout                    |
| Detail        | Clean realistic texture and crisp detail at native resolution; no soft upscaled small images                |
| Focal subject | Keep the main subject in the central 70% of the canvas; leave room around important edges                   |
| Text          | No typography, watermark, logos, signs, fabricated business branding or embedded captions                   |
| Cropping      | Do not pre-crop into portrait, square or social sizes. We will create responsive web and social derivatives |
| File handling | Deliver these PNGs together in one ZIP; do not convert them to AVIF/WebP beforehand                         |

**Every image must be the same 2048 × 1536 canvas.** A 2048-wide image with a different height does not meet this brief. Do not stretch another shape to fit: compose or regenerate it at 4:3. In this brief, “2K” means 2048 pixels on the long edge; it does not mean a 2048 × 2048 square.

## Shared visual direction

Photorealistic editorial architectural imagery for a Cape Town construction and renovation website. Restrained, believable residential scale; warm off-white plaster, natural timber, muted green garden planting and honest concrete or masonry. Neutral daylight, controlled highlights, natural shadows and a consistent white balance. Straight architectural verticals, realistic perspective and an approximately 35–50 mm lens feel. Clear compositions with one primary subject and limited clutter.

The images should look like a coherent architectural editorial series. Avoid dramatically different lighting, extreme saturation, excessive HDR, glossy luxury-property advertising and artificial depth-of-field effects. Keep materials and detailing credible rather than spectacular. Avoid identifiable addresses, vehicle registration plates, client identities and recognisable landmark properties.

Apply this direction to every prompt below. The prompts describe illustrative scenes; they make no claim that the pictured work was performed by Regardin.
