The live interaction audit reports zero page or HTTP failures. External contact links were clicked with navigation intercepted to avoid placing calls or sending messages; distinct internal destinations were subsequently followed normally. HTTPS responses used a TLS-verified curl bridge for Chromium because of the execution environment's browser-network restrictions. Live brief copying and portfolio copying were verified directly in the browser. Native sharing, permission refusal and optional account responses use controlled synthetic tests.

Local browser verification: 55 full-suite checks plus one targeted keyboard/dialog check, all passed (56 checks total). Build, 24-route metadata/link checks, HTML validation, banned-copy and lint gates pass. Complete-grid and overflow tests still cover six viewport widths. cPanel packaging was rebuilt and its checksum verified; no application or styling change was needed.

Remaining setup is explicit: Formspree endpoint and mailbox receipt, Google Analytics/Search Console identifiers and property verification, active WhatsApp delivery and cPanel production acceptance. The beta offers phone, WhatsApp and email preparation while direct submission is unconfigured. No enquiries, emails, messages, advertisements or campaigns were sent in this audit.
